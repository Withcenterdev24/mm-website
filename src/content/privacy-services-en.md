### English: Website and support

Notice updated: October 9, 2026. Withcenter handles inquiries at
[thruthesky@gmail.com](mailto:thruthesky@gmail.com). This notice covers visits to this website and information
you send us, separately from the offline game.

#### Website requests and cookies

The public website uses Vercel hosting. Vercel processes technical request information, including IP address,
system configuration and approximate city/country derived from IP, to deliver and operate its services.
See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).

The website code does not set cookies, use browser local storage, or include analytics, advertising, tracking
scripts or third-party embeds. It has no account registration or hosted contact form. Hosting security features
may process additional technical information or present a challenge when needed to protect the service.

#### Support emails and their purpose

Emailing us is optional. We receive your email address, any name your email includes, message contents,
attachments and email metadata. A screenshot or diagnostic report may contain personal information, so remove
anything unnecessary before sending it. We use your message to answer your inquiry and investigate the problem
you describe. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

The support address uses Google's Gmail service, which processes and stores email. Your own email provider also
handles delivery. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Contacting support is not
required to play the game.

#### Retention and deletion

We retain support messages and attachments only while needed to resolve the inquiry and related follow-up,
or meet an applicable legal obligation or handle a dispute. Once those purposes no longer require the information,
we delete it. You can request earlier deletion at the contact address above. If we must keep some information,
we will explain the reason when responding.

Hosting records and provider backup copies have separate retention and deletion processes described in the
providers' notices. Deleting an email from our mailbox does not remove copies in your mailbox, your email
provider, or all provider backups immediately. There is no Withcenter player database to erase.

#### Sharing, international processing and security

Vercel handles website requests and Google handles support email as described above. Information may also be
disclosed where required by law or necessary to address a security incident or legal claim. These providers may
process information outside your country; their notices describe international processing and protections.
The game does not send saved progress or settings to these providers.

The public website is served over HTTPS. Email and internet communications cannot be guaranteed completely
secure. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a support message.

#### Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing
of, object to processing of, or receive a portable copy of your personal information. Where processing relies on
consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to your local
data-protection authority.

To make a request, email [thruthesky@gmail.com](mailto:thruthesky@gmail.com), ideally from the address used for
the original inquiry. Explain the request and identify the relevant conversation; do not send an identity document
unless we explain why additional verification is necessary. We will respond within the time required by applicable
law. We cannot remotely retrieve your local game saves or delete a device backup on your behalf.

Where GDPR or equivalent rules apply, our basis for answering support inquiries and operating a secure website
is our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the service, subject to your rights. Legal
obligations may require particular records to be retained. If a future activity requires consent, we will request
it separately. We do not use this website to make automated decisions about you or profile you for advertising.

#### Children and parents

Offline gameplay does not collect personal information from children. For support concerning a child, a parent
or guardian should contact us and provide only what is needed to explain the issue. Children should not send
personal details, photos or recordings of themselves. If you believe a child sent us personal information,
contact us to request review and deletion. We will delete it unless an applicable law requires retention.

#### Changes and external services

We will update this notice and its date when our practices change. If a change requires notice or consent under
applicable law, we will provide that before the new processing starts. Store links and other external links take
you to services with their own privacy policies.
