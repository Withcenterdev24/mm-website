## Magnet Mayhem: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Magnet Mayhem (Android package `com.withcenter.magnetmayhem`, iOS bundle ID `com.withcenter.magnetmayhem`) is a game
made by Withcenter. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. It applies to everyone,
including children.

### We collect no personal information

- The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information: no name, e-mail, phone number, account,
  location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID.
- The game has no sign-in, no chat, no user-generated content and no links to social networks.
- The game shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
- The game contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- The game asks for no device permissions and works fully offline. It does not send anything over the internet.

### What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked stages) and your settings (language, sound, camera sensitivity) in a file on
your own device. This file never leaves the device, and we cannot read it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

### Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from
children under 13 either, and parental consent is not needed for data collection.

### App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information when you download or update the game (for example, crash
reports you choose to share). That is handled by their own privacy policies, not by this game.

### Changes

If the game ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change.
