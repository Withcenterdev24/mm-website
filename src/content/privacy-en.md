## Magnet Mayhem: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Magnet Mayhem (Android package `com.withcenter.magnetmayhem`, iOS bundle ID `com.withcenter.magnetmayhem`) is a game
made by Withcenter. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. It applies to everyone,
including children. Withcenter is the publisher and the contact for privacy questions. This section covers the
installed game. The [website and support notice](#website) below covers website visits and emails separately.

### We collect no personal information

- The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information: no name, e-mail, phone number, account,
  location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID.
- The game has no sign-in, no chat, no user-generated content and no links to social networks.
- The game shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
- The game contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- The game does not request access to sensitive device features such as location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  It works fully offline and does not transmit gameplay or saved data to Withcenter or another service.

### What stays on your device

The game saves progress (unlocked stages and whether you answered the initial training question or started training) and settings in its
local app storage. Settings include language, left/right-handed controls, keyboard bindings, touch camera
sensitivity, graphics quality and the performance-overlay preference. These files let the game remember your
choices between sessions. They do not contain an account, contact details or a player identifier, and the game
does not upload them. Withcenter cannot remotely access them.

### Retention, deletion and device backups

Local progress and settings remain until the app's data is removed. On Android, clearing the game's app storage
in device settings removes these files. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it on iOS) removes its local
app data. These actions reset your saved progress and preferences. There is no Withcenter account or server-side
player record to delete, and there is no in-game data-collection consent to withdraw.

Your operating system or a backup service may include app data in a device backup, depending on platform and
settings. For example, [iCloud Backup can include app data](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770). Removing local
data does not necessarily delete existing backups, and restoring a backup may restore the saved files. Manage
backup copies through your device or backup provider. Withcenter does not operate that backup service.

### Children

The game's no-personal-information collection practices apply to children, including children under 13. Playing
does not require a name, email address, date of birth or account. Website visits and support emails are separate
from offline gameplay; see the children's section of the [website and support notice](#website).

### App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information when you download or update the game (for example, crash
reports you choose to share). The game does not include its own crash-reporting SDK. Store and operating-system
services handle their information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you choose to send us a report or screenshot,
the website and support notice applies to the information we receive.

### Changes

If the game ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change.
